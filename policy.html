Community Policy

Pre-release draft. These are the project's intended community principles, not a final SDK license or contractual EULA. They may change before release.

We want modders to be free to create — and to know which parts of Starlight Studio they can extend.

Community extensions

We intend to support separately developed plugins, custom tools, UI themes and integrations through a documented SDK. The publicly released SDK components will have their own license, which has not been selected yet. Until then, this page does not grant rights to proprietary application code.

Keep attribution clear

Independent plugins may identify themselves as “A community extension for Starlight Studio.” They must not claim to be official. Any separately authorized redistribution of a modified application containing Starlight proprietary components will have to credit Starlight Studio and clearly label itself as an unofficial modified version. No general right to redistribute those components is granted here.

Your vehicle mods remain yours

We do not require Starlight Studio credit merely because you use the application to create an original vehicle mod. You retain the rights you hold in your own work. Any third-party model, game data, trademark or library remains subject to its own license.

Respect users

Community extensions should be transparent about their functionality, data access and dependencies. They must not impersonate official releases or mislead users about security updates or endorsement.

Contributions and permissions

Plugin authors generally retain rights in independently created extension code. The terms for submissions to official SDK repositories will be specified when those repositories launch. Starlight Studio core remains closed source.

Questions and updates will be handled through the project's public information repository.
